Chroma Lab is a puzzle game that works entirely on your device.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, transmit or share personal information, and it does not connect to the internet.

**What stays on your device:** your progress (levels completed, stars, coins, achievements, collection items), your settings (sound, music, vibration, accessibility options) and the level you are currently playing. This is stored locally so the game can resume. Deleting the app, or using Settings > Reset progress, removes it.

**Permissions:** the app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

**Advertising and analytics:** the app contains no advertising and no analytics. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and you will be told in the app.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
